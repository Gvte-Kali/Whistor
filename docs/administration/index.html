    Web administration and users

    Choose your access method

    Method Open on your PC Requirements
    LAN, without a VPN http://SERVER_LAN_IP:8080 Same network or LAN routing; firewall allowing TCP 8080
    Direct Tailscale http://SERVER_TAILSCALE_IP:8080 Both devices on the same tailnet; policy allowing TCP 8080
    Tailscale Serve HTTPS URL printed by Tailscale Serve/HTTPS enabled for your tailnet
    SSH http://localhost:8080 after opening a tunnel SSH access to the server
    Internet without a VPN Your HTTPS domain An HTTPS reverse proxy on the server

    LAN: the direct route

    The service listens on 0.0.0.0:8080 by default. On your PC, open http://192.168.1.42:8080, replacing the example address with your server's IP. No Matrix proxy or Tor configuration is needed for administration.

    Your server firewall and network rules must allow the connection. Allow the networks you intend to use; Whistor does not modify your firewall automatically.

    Direct Tailscale access

    Install Tailscale on both the server and your PC, join the same tailnet, then check the server's address:

    tailscale ip -4

    On your PC, open http://THE_PRINTED_ADDRESS:8080. Tailnet policies must allow your PC to reach that port. No public port forwarding on your router is required.

    Tailscale Serve with HTTPS

    On the server:

    tailscale serve --bg http://127.0.0.1:8080
tailscale serve status

    Open the printed HTTPS URL from your PC connected to the tailnet. Tailscale may ask you to enable Serve and HTTPS. This uses Serve, not Funnel: access stays private to your tailnet.

    For access exclusively through this method, set WHISTOR_ADMIN_BIND=127.0.0.1 in .env and recreate the service.

    SSH tunnel

    Run this on your PC and keep the terminal open:

    ssh -N -L 8080:127.0.0.1:8080 USER@SERVER

    Then open http://localhost:8080. If your local port is already in use:

    ssh -N -L 18080:127.0.0.1:8080 USER@SERVER

    In that case, open http://localhost:18080 in your browser.

    Change the port or bind address

    In .env, next to docker-compose.yml:

    WHISTOR_ADMIN_BIND=0.0.0.0
WHISTOR_ADMIN_PORT=8080
    Bind address Effect
    0.0.0.0 All IPv4 interfaces, including LAN and Tailscale
    Server's LAN IP Only that interface
    Server's Tailscale IP Only that interface; it must exist when the service starts
    127.0.0.1 Local access, SSH, Tailscale Serve or a local reverse proxy

    Apply changes on the server:

    docker compose up -d --build admin

    Update your URLs and tunnel commands if you change the port.

    Manage accounts

    1. Sign in with a Synapse administrator account, separate from your Linux login.
    2. Search for an account in the users list or create a new one.
    3. Change its display name, password or privileges as needed.
    4. Deactivate an account to remove access. Deactivation does not automatically mean that all associated data is erased.
    5. Keep at least one working administrator and sign out when finished.

    Synapse checks permissions: an ordinary user cannot call the administration APIs, even if they can open the login page.

    Internet access without a VPN

    Put an HTTPS reverse proxy in front of http://127.0.0.1:8080. Use a dedicated hostname at /, preserve the Host header and send X-Forwarded-Proto: https. Deploying under a URL subpath is not supported.

    Do not send administrator credentials over the Internet using HTTP; use HTTPS. Publishing this proxy also makes the relayed Matrix APIs reachable at that address. Choose network access rules accordingly.
